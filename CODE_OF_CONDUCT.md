# Code of Conduct

auto-hwp welcomes people who want Korean documents to be more open, programmable, and safe to work with. We expect every
project space to stay respectful and useful, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and community events.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and code with concrete evidence; do not attack people.
- Respect different experience levels, languages, identities, and accessibility needs.
- Protect user documents and personal information. Use synthetic or explicitly redistributable fixtures.
- Accept a maintainer's request to narrow scope, move a support question, or remove sensitive material.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and give credit for others' work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate misgendering, doxxing, credential disclosure,
publication of confidential documents, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

For non-security conduct concerns, contact a maintainer through the repository without publishing sensitive details. For a
confidential report, use [GitHub's private security advisory form](https://github.com/kwakseongjae/auto-hwp/security/advisories/new).
Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock a thread, reject a contribution, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Decisions should consider context, impact, and a pattern of behavior, and should protect the reporter's
privacy as far as practicable.

This policy applies whenever someone represents the project or participates in a project-managed space.
